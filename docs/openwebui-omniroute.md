To connect Open WebUI with Omniroute

* Go to **administration**, then **settings** and **connections**
* Enable **API OpenAI** 
* Click on the gear icon and change the OpenAI URL to: **http://omniroute:20128/api/v1**
* Add the omniroute apikey to the **Bearer** field
* Finally, click save.
